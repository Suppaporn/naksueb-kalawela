# Audio provenance

The interface effects and jungle music are generated by this project. The five MP4 scene videos were supplied by the project owner and contain their own audio tracks.

Music, ambience, and interface effects are synthesized in the browser at runtime with the Web Audio API in `index.html`. The jungle-adventure motif uses newly arranged oscillator notes and synthesized percussion. Wind, water, birds, insects, fire crackle, stone tapping, button clicks, and the evidence-complete celebration (including clap-like sounds) are generated from oscillator tones and generated noise; there are no embedded or downloaded audio samples.

There are no downloaded audio samples for the Web Audio sounds. Rights to the user-supplied video soundtracks should be checked separately before public reuse. The guide GIFs are also supplied by the project owner and do not contain audio.
